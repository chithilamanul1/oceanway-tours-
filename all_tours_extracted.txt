This specialized expedition avoids commercial mass tourism by traversing the pristine
northwest, eastern seaboard, and central reservoir valleys. From the leopards and
natural lakes of Wilpattu to the coral gardens of Pigeon Island, the serene shallow bay
of Pasikudah, the swimming elephants of Gal Oya, and the hill capital of Kandy.
KEY EXPEDITION MILESTONES:
📍 Wilpattu National Park: Untamed leopards & sand-rimmed natural willus
📍 Trincomalee & Pigeon Island: Coral reef snorkeling with turtles & reef sharks
📍 Pasikudah Bay: Crystal-clear turquoise water & reef-protected shallows
📍 Gal Oya National Park: Unique reservoir boat safari tracking swimming elephants
📍 Bintenne & Mahiyanganaya: Indigenous Vedda chieftain forest walk
📍 Kandy: Temple of the Tooth Relic & Royal Botanic Gardens
